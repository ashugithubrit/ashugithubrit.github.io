📋 What lemo-stampy Does

lemo-stampy is a small helper that sits quietly in your Mac menu bar — the row of little icons at the top right of your screen. It has one clear job: adding a visible stamp to PDF files. The default stamp text reads “For XX use only,” and you can change the “XX” part to whatever wording fits your situation, such as a team name, a project label, a client name, or a short internal note.

The app does not ask you to learn new software. It works alongside Preview, the free PDF reader that already comes with every Mac. You open a PDF the way you normally would, click the lemo-stampy icon in the menu bar, and the app produces a new stamped copy of the document. Your original file is left untouched, which means you always keep a clean version for your own records while sharing a clearly marked version with others.

Everything happens on your own computer. There is no account to sign up for, no cloud service in the middle, and no upload step. Your documents never leave your machine. That makes lemo-stampy a good fit for contracts, invoices, drafts, internal reports, design proofs, and any paperwork you want to label as “confidential,” “internal use,” “for review,” or “sample.”

🖱️ How to Use It Day to Day

The routine is intentionally short. Open the document you want to mark in Preview. Click the stamp icon in the menu bar. Type or confirm the wording you want in place of “XX” — for example “Finance Team” or “Client Review.” Let the app create the stamped copy. Then share, email, or file the copy as usual, keeping the clean original wherever you normally store it.

Because the stamp lands on a copy, you can repeat the process for different audiences without ever damaging your source file. Many people keep one untouched master PDF and generate separate stamped versions for different recipients — one marked for internal circulation, another marked for a specific client. This keeps your records tidy and your sharing habits consistent.

💡 Good Habits to Know

  • Keep the copy separate. Save stamped copies in a folder that clearly says what they are, so you never confuse a marked version with your untouched original.
  • Use short stamp text. A few words read best on a page. Long sentences can crowd the layout, especially on documents with narrow margins.
  • Check the copy before sharing. Open the stamped file once to confirm the wording and placement look right for the recipient.
  • Let the app start with your Mac. If you use lemo-stampy often, let it open automatically at login so the icon is always waiting in the menu bar.
  • Keep Preview handy. lemo-stampy is designed to work together with Preview, so keeping your PDFs opening in Preview makes the flow smooth.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

  • The icon is not in the menu bar. Open the app again from your Applications folder and give it a moment to start. If your menu bar is very full, close a few other menu bar items so the new icon has room.
  • The download did not finish. Return to the project page and start the download again. Make sure you have enough free space on your Mac before saving.
  • The app will not open. Move it into your Applications folder and try again. Confirm the one-time safety prompt if macOS shows it.
  • I cannot find the stamped copy. Look in the same folder as your original PDF. Stamped copies are usually saved next to the file you were working on.
  • The stamp wording looks wrong. Reopen the stamp options from the menu bar icon and adjust the text, then create a fresh copy.
